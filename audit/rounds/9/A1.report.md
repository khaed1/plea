# Audit report

> PLEA security audit, round 9, area A1: The game: gate, Tribunal, seasons. PLEA is a sell-gated meme token on Uniswap v4 paired with IMD: anyone buys, selling needs a plea judged by a 30-seat IdentityMD oracle panel, approved pleas score season points for IMD and PLEA prizes, and every trade pays cashback in staked IMD through imd/acc's Stacker. Solidity 0.8.26, Foundry (cancun, via-IR), a Uniswap v4 hook, Intake v2 callbacks. > Context: CabalGate takes pleas and appeals, computes the fact score and the need from the hook's hourly checkpoints, pays the 0.5 IMD fee to the Intake with the question, checks the signed verdict (OracleAttestation: EIP-712 then ERC-1271, panel 30, quorum 17, score 0-45) in the Intake's callback, and runs the 7-minute gated sale through the hook; the dead-man (33 hours without a verdict) ends the game. The Tribunal is Objection!: for 20 minutes after an approval a second panel may re-score the plea (0.5 IMD fee + 0.35 IMD stake), sustained when newScore + 2 < need, otherwise overruled, void on no answer; at most two objections per plea, from different wallets, and both may be pending at once. Seasons records each approved plea's min(original, lowest re-score) as points, times 1.2 when the seller holds an IMD seat NFT at record time, keeps each wallet's best three, pays the top 25 with 60+ points from 450 down to 350 bps of the season pot and the 10,000,000 PLEA bonus in each of the first six seasons, excludes the owner and the launch owner, and flushes what is left to the buy wall after the death.

## Findings

### 1. Low: After the pass mark moved 71 -> 69, a large same-day sale at a profit is no longer refused (fact 24 -> need 45 is accepted), contrary to README's 'cannot pass'

`src/CabalGate.sol:434`

```
if (need > MAX_PLEA_SCORE) revert CannotPass(need);
```

README.md line 90 still says 'A large same-day sale at a profit cannot pass.' Under the old pass mark that was true for every case: the best large (>35%) same-day profit plea scores share 3 + hold 0 + gain<=50% 12 + 24h trend up 9 = 24, and 71 - 24 = 47 > 45, so _open refused it with CannotPass before the 0.5 IMD fee. With PASS_MARK 69 (the owner's agreed change) the same facts give need 45, which `need > MAX_PLEA_SCORE` lets through: the plea is accepted and paid for, and a panel score of 45 approves a same-day sale of up to 50% of the balance (max 2.5M PLEA) at a profit. The arithmetic is the agreed table, so the code and the agreed numbers agree; what is wrong is the README's promise, a bound that assumed the old pass mark. This is the only corner affected (trend flat gives 20 -> need 49, still refused; a loss is not a profit). No funds are at risk beyond the seller's own fee, and passing needs the maximum panel score. Fix: change the README sentence (for example 'a large same-day sale at a profit needs a perfect 45 and only in a rising market'), or, if the owner wants the old guarantee back, move the CannotPass bound. That changes an agreed number, so it is the owner's call. Merged here: audit_math's report on the new CannotPass regions for week-long holders (fact 23 -> need 46 where runs 7-9 gave 27 -> 44). I checked it and it is arithmetically right, but no current document promises those states can pass, so it adds no separate defect. It belongs in the same doc update: the README could list the states that are refused before payment.
