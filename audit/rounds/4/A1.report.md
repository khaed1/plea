# Audit report

> PLEA security audit, round 4, area A1: The game: gate, Tribunal, seasons. PLEA is a sell-gated meme token on Uniswap v4 paired with IMD: anyone buys, selling needs a plea judged by a 30-seat IdentityMD oracle panel, approved pleas score season points for IMD and PLEA prizes, and every trade pays cashback in staked IMD through imd/acc's Stacker. Solidity 0.8.26, Foundry (cancun, via-IR), a Uniswap v4 hook, IdentityMD Intake v2 callbacks. This is the mainnet code, rehearsed first on Sepolia (launch.json on this commit has the Sepolia settings). Other areas of the same commit are audited by separate jobs; stay on this one.

> Context: CabalGate takes pleas and appeals, computes the fact score and the need from the hook's hourly checkpoints, pays the 0.5 IMD fee to the Intake with the question, checks the signed verdict (OracleAttestation: EIP-712 then ERC-1271, panel 30, quorum 17, score 0-45) in the Intake's callback, and runs the 7-minute gated sale through the hook; the dead-man (33 hours without a verdict) ends the game. The Tribunal is Objection!: for 20 minutes after an approval a second panel may re-score the plea (0.5 IMD fee + 0.35 IMD stake), sustained when newScore + 2 < need, otherwise overruled, void on no answer; at most two rulings per plea from different wallets. Seasons records each approved plea's min(original, lowest re-score) as points, times 1.2 when the seller holds an IMD seat NFT at record time (run 7), keeps each wallet's best three, pays the top 25 with 60+ points from 450 down to 350 bps of the season pot and the 10,000,000 PLEA bonus in each of the first six seasons, excludes the owner and the launch owner, and flushes what is left to the buy wall after the death.

## Findings

### 1. Low: The 24h trend reference has no two-day bound: price24hAgo serves any older checkpoint left in the ring, while the README and THREAT-MODEL say the reference is 'within two days' / 'survives up to 23 qu

`src/PleaHook.sol:297`

```
            if (c.priceX96 != 0 && c.hour <= target && c.hour >= bestHour) {
```

README.md line 118 states the trend band's reference is 'the checkpoint from 24 hours ago, or the newest older one within two days', and THREAT-MODEL section 3 says the day-old reference 'survives up to 23 quiet hours'. The scan in price24hAgo (read by CabalGate._factScoreAt at src/CabalGate.sol:280) accepts any slot whose hour is at or below target with no lower bound: a checkpoint written 100 hours ago stays in its slot until a trade happens in the same hour-of-ring (every 48 hours) and is then served as the day-old reference. After 48 or more quiet hours (no trade at all, which the Cabal survives since verdicts alone keep it alive and a denied site plea makes no trade) the gate scores the trend band (up 9 / flat 5 / down 0) against a reference several days old instead of the documented 'no history' (5). Effect at most 5 need points, only in a market quiet for two days, and nobody can choose the stale reference except by timing a plea, so this is an edge case with no realistic loss (Low), not a changed agreed number. Reproduced by the judge (test below) and by the economics specialist. Fix: either document the actual rule (the newest checkpoint at or before 24 hours ago that is still in the 48-slot ring, however old) in README and THREAT-MODEL section 3, or add `c.hour + RING > target` to the scan (and `t.hour == target` already covers the exact slot) so references older than two days read as no history. Merged duplicate: audit_economics 747f3565.

**Reproduction**

test/scratch/JudgeA1.t.sol test_referenceOlderThanTwoDaysIsServedAsTheDayOldPrice (passes on this code, i.e. shows the behaviour): alice buys 50 IMD in hour h0 (checkpoint p0 = the price found), bob buys 500 IMD in the same hour (price lifted, no new checkpoint); no trade until hour h0+90, where carol's 1 IMD buy checkpoints p1 > 1.02 p0; in hour h0+100 hook.price24hAgo() returns p0 (ring slot h0%48 still holds hour h0, 100 hours old) and gate.factScore(alice, 10% of balance) equals 16 + 4 + pnl + 9: the trend band scores 'up' (+9) against a 100-hour-old reference. Expected per README/THREAT-MODEL: a reference older than two days is 'no history' (+5), need 4 points higher.

### 2. Info: OBJECTIONS.md promises objections and rulings after the Cabal's death, a state the contracts make unreachable

`OBJECTIONS.md:78`

```
**After the Cabal dies:** objections and rulings still work for pleas approved before the death. They only affect the last season, which closes no sooner than one hour after the death.
```

Every verdict sets CabalGate.lastVerdictAt (src/CabalGate.sol:530), so the death (diedAt = lastVerdictAt + 33 h) is at least 33 hours after the newest approval. Tribunal.object refuses any plea older than OBJECTION_WINDOW = 20 minutes (src/Tribunal.sol:245) and a ruling is refused TooLate more than 20 minutes after filing (src/Tribunal.sol:292), so every window and every pending objection has ended at least 32 h 20 min before the death. THREAT-MODEL invariants 6, 12 and 13 and the code agree; only this paragraph (which THREAT-MODEL section 3 incorporates by reference through the Known limits and Decisions) describes behaviour that cannot occur, and could mislead a keeper or test plan around post-death rulings. Documentation only; no code effect. Fix: replace the paragraph with what holds: the last objection window closes at least 32 h 40 min before the death; only settle (and expire of a stale pending objection) can run after it; a late overruled stake becomes rollover and, after the last close, reaches the wall through a second flushToWall. Merged duplicate: audit_economics 3699614e.

**Reproduction**

test/scratch/JudgeA1.t.sol test_noObjectionCanBeFiledAfterTheDeath (passes on this code, i.e. shows the behaviour): alice's plea is approved at V; after 33 hours gate.cabalAlive() is false and diedAt() is nonzero; bob's Tribunal.object(id, text) reverts WindowClosed(id). Expected per OBJECTIONS.md line 78: the objection is accepted after the death. Actual: refused, because V + 20 min < V + 33 h = diedAt for every approval.
