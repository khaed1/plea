PLEA security audit, round 6, area A1: The game: gate, Tribunal, seasons. PLEA is a sell-gated meme token on Uniswap v4 paired with IMD: anyone buys, selling needs a plea judged by a 30-seat IdentityMD oracle panel, approved pleas score season points for IMD and PLEA prizes, and every trade pays cashback in staked IMD through imd/acc's Stacker. Solidity 0.8.26, Foundry (cancun, via-IR), a Uniswap v4 hook, IdentityMD Intake v2 callbacks. This is the mainnet code, rehearsed first on Sepolia (launch.json on this commit has the Sepolia settings). Other areas of the same commit are audited by separate jobs; stay on this one.

Context: CabalGate takes pleas and appeals, computes the fact score and the need from the hook's hourly checkpoints, pays the 0.5 IMD fee to the Intake with the question, checks the signed verdict (OracleAttestation: EIP-712 then ERC-1271, panel 30, quorum 17, score 0-45) in the Intake's callback, and runs the 7-minute gated sale through the hook; the dead-man (33 hours without a verdict) ends the game. The Tribunal is Objection!: for 20 minutes after an approval a second panel may re-score the plea (0.5 IMD fee + 0.35 IMD stake), sustained when newScore + 2 < need, otherwise overruled, void on no answer; at most two objections per plea, from different wallets, and both may be pending at once. Seasons records each approved plea's min(original, lowest re-score) as points, times 1.2 when the seller holds an IMD seat NFT at record time (run 7), keeps each wallet's best three, pays the top 25 with 60+ points from 450 down to 350 bps of the season pot and the 10,000,000 PLEA bonus in each of the first six seasons, excludes the owner and the launch owner, and flushes what is left to the buy wall after the death.
Changed since round 5 (FINDINGS.md "Changes after round 5"): R5-A1-1 fixed: two pending slots (Case.pending[2]); a second wallet files while the first objection is pending; rulings + pending at most two; no wallet twice (ruled or pending); a sustain closes the case to new objections, one already pending is still ruled; stale pending ones are voided on filing. R5-A1-2 fixed: exact profit band edges. Verify both, and the 40-minute hold. Earlier: R1-R5-A1-*.
Look hardest at:
- The verdict path: can any callback (signed answer, unsigned failure, replay, a second answer, a wrong request id, an answer after expiry or after the death) move value, revive the Cabal, approve a plea that should be denied, or leave a plea stuck?
- The sale: selling without an approval, a different amount, outside the window, twice, through another route, or a transfer that escapes the gate while the Cabal lives; the fee sale for letters (submitFor / appealFor / executeSellFor: only the Letterbox).
- Fact score and need: hourly checkpoints vs spot, the share bands and the lesser-share rule for appeals, hold time and P&L from the hook's basis, rounding, the 1% / 50% / 2.5M bounds, CannotPass.
- Objection! economics and state: the two-objection cap, the two pending slots (a rival beside a sham, one wallet twice, a third while two wait, a sustain while one waits, voids and stale slots in any order), stake conservation across sustained / overruled / void / expired, checkpointObjection's season booking and UnfundedStake, rulings during and after the death, the hold vs the close grace (40 minutes inside 60).
- Seasons: record once, held / sustained refusals, the best-three replacement and tie keys, the top-25 insertion, shareBps sum, pot vs accountedPot (claims redeemed through unlockCallback), rollover, the bonus burn, the owner exclusion at record and at close, flushToWall, and every fail-open read (Tribunal, launcher, hook owner, the seat NFT with its 100k gas cap): can any of them block a record or a close, or be made to lie?
- The seat multiplier: rounding, the uint8 / uint16 bounds (45 x 1.2 = 54 per plea, 162 for three), a seat borrowed for one transaction (accepted, THREAT-MODEL section 3), a malicious seat contract.
