> Context: CabalGate takes pleas and appeals, computes the fact score and the need from the hook's hourly checkpoints, pays the 0.5 IMD fee to the Intake with the question, checks the signed verdict (OracleAttestation: EIP-712 then ERC-1271, panel 30, quorum 17, score 0-45) in the Intake's callback, and runs the 7-minute gated sale through the hook; the dead-man (33 hours without a verdict) ends the game. The Tribunal is Objection!: for 20 minutes after an approval a second panel may re-score the plea (0.5 IMD fee + 0.35 IMD stake), sustained when newScore + 2 < need, otherwise overruled, void on no answer; at most two objections per plea, from different wallets, and both may be pending at once. Seasons records each approved plea's min(original, lowest re-score) as points, times 1.2 when the seller holds an IMD seat NFT at record time (run 7), keeps each wallet's best three, pays the top 25 with 60+ points from 450 down to 350 bps of the season pot and the 10,000,000 PLEA bonus in each of the first six seasons, excludes the owner and the launch owner, and flushes what is left to the buy wall after the death.

## Findings

### 1. Low: Tribunal.status reports an overruled plea's raw score as its points, ignoring the seat multiplier and the points Seasons actually recorded

`src/Tribunal.sol:237`

```
                Strings.toString(c.lowest < score ? c.lowest : score),
```

The explorer line `Tribunal.status(pleaId)` ends an overruled case with `· counts N points`, where N = min(original score, lowest re-score). Since run 7 the points a plea counts are that score times 1.2 (rounded down) when the seller holds an IMD seat NFT at record time (`Seasons.pointsFor`, stored in `Seasons.pointsOf(pleaId)` once recorded). The Tribunal's line never reads either, so for every seat holder it states a points figure that is wrong before and after the recording. The same mismatch was fixed for the Letterbox's status line in R1-A2-5 (it reads `Seasons.pointsOf` once recorded); the Tribunal's line was not updated when the multiplier arrived. Display only: nothing on chain reads `status`, `Seasons.record` and `pointsOf` are correct (invariant 7 holds), and the Tribunal's sustain comparison correctly uses the unmultiplied score. Severity Low per THREAT-MODEL section 4 (display text). Merged from the audit_flow specialist's finding; reproduced against the code with the fixture below. Fix: once `seasons.recorded(pleaId)`, print `Seasons.pointsOf(pleaId)`; before that, print the score with the word `scores` (or `pointsFor(seller, min)` marked provisional), as the Letterbox does after R1-A2-5.

**Reproduction**

Fixture with the Tribunal deployed (test/utils/Fixture.sol, withTribunal() = true). seat.mint(alice) so Seasons.holdsSeat(alice) is true. alice's plea is approved at score 40 (need 35 in the fixture). bob objects; the panel re-scores 38 (38 + 2 >= 35, so OVERRULED, case lowest = 38). skip(20 minutes + 1); anyone calls seasons.record(pleaId): Seasons.pointsOf(pleaId) == 45 (38 * 12000 / 10000) and bestOf(0, alice)[0] == 45. Expected: tribunal.status(pleaId) reports 45 points (or the recorded pointsOf). Actual (logged from the probe test): `Plea #1 · objections closed · OVERRULED (38 vs need 35) · counts 38 points`. The same line is shown before the recording, when the provisional figure for a seat holder would also be 45.
