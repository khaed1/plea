PLEA security audit, round 6, area A2: Letters and the launch: Letterbox, PleaLaunch, PLEA, PleaDistributor. PLEA is a sell-gated meme token on Uniswap v4 paired with IMD: anyone buys, selling needs a plea judged by a 30-seat IdentityMD oracle panel, approved pleas score season points for IMD and PLEA prizes, and every trade pays cashback in staked IMD through imd/acc's Stacker. Solidity 0.8.26, Foundry (cancun, via-IR), a Uniswap v4 hook, IdentityMD Intake v2 callbacks. Context: the Letterbox turns a transaction's input data (or letter(text)) into a plea for 20% of the writer's PLEA, an APPEAL, a SELL or a NAME; it escrows the PLEA, pays the fees in PLEA through the gate's fee sale (0.5 IMD oracle fee, 0.25 IMD runner tip, an appeal's 0.35 IMD wall share; at most the snapshot value + 10% and 10% of the balance), and anyone delivers an approved plea for at least 95% of its snapshot value for the tip; the site's auto-sell box (plead / appealAuto) does the same and pays the 0.35 IMD auto-sell fee from a delivered sale. PLEA is the ERC-20 with transfer gating while the Cabal lives (system senders and the owner's add-only allowlist exempt; two-step ownership), the one-time launcher registration and the one-time mint 82 / 6 / 12. PleaLaunch registers the system at launch, commits to the hook's, the Letterbox's and the Tribunal's creation code, deploys them afterwards for anyone, and opens trading (seed, with the mainnet clock check). PleaDistributor is the Merkle claim for the 12%.
