## Findings

### 1. Medium: A seller with mixed holdings picks the 'no buy record' band by pleading one wei over the buy record (up to 6 need points; a CannotPass plea becomes passable)

`src/CabalGate.sol:270`

```
        bool covered = heldPlea >= amount;
```

Specialist (audit_flow) finding, reproduced by the judge against the code; kept at Medium. `_factScoreAt` decides the hold-time and profit facts for the whole plea from one all-or-nothing comparison, `covered = heldPlea >= amount`. A covered plea is scored on the seller's real basis (buy-weighted hold time, exact P&L band 13/12/10/8/6/4); an uncovered one is scored as 'PLEA no buy record covers': held since `startedAt` and profit 10 (THREAT-MODEL invariant 3, section 3 'Prize PLEA and claimed distributor PLEA'). Because the seller chooses `amount`, any wallet that holds both PLEA with a buy record and PLEA without one (a season prize, a distributor claim, an allowlisted transfer) can choose the branch: plead `heldPlea` and be scored on its history, or plead `heldPlea + 1` and be scored as if none of it had a history. Once the launch is a week old the uncovered branch gives 7 + 10 = 17 fact points, which beats every covered outcome except an old holder at a loss (7 + 13): an early buyer at a gain over 1,000% gains 6 need points (4 -> 10), a fresh buyer at a gain under 50% gains 5 (0 + 12 -> 7 + 10), a fresh buyer at a loss gains 4 (0 + 13 -> 7 + 10). The share band, the trend band and the sale are unchanged, so this is a free choice of the better P&L / hold facts for a plea the record covers almost entirely. Precondition: unrecorded PLEA of at least the recorded amount plus 2 wei (the 50% ceiling), which the 12% distributor allocation and every season prize create. No agreed number changes; the design's 'uncovered' rule is applied to a plea that is 99.9999% covered, which section 3 does not describe. Severity Medium on the THREAT-MODEL scale (a wrong verdict path, bounded effect, costs the seller nothing): larger than R3-A1-1 (5 points, Medium). Minimal fix keeping the agreed numbers: score the covered part on the record and the uncovered part on the 'no record' rule and take the PLEA-weighted result, or treat a plea as covered when `heldPlea != 0` (the dust-buy concern is already handled by `since`/`spent` being that record's own), or require the record to cover a high share (e.g. 90%) of the plea; THREAT-MODEL invariant 3 / section 3 should then say how a partly covered plea is scored. THREAT-MODEL invariants checked by the judge on this path: 3 (fact score from hourly checkpoints, never spot: holds; the bands and numbers are the agreed ones), 2 (amount bounds hold for both pleas), 4 (the verdict binding is untouched).

```solidity
// SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT
pragma solidity 0.8.26;

import {Fixture} from "../utils/Fixture.sol";
import {CabalGate} from "../../src/CabalGate.sol";

/// @notice A seller who holds PLEA with a buy record at a large gain plus PLEA with no buy record
/// (a prize, a distributor claim) can plead one wei more than the record covers and be scored on
/// the "no buy record" branch (held since trading opened, profit 10) instead of their real profit
/// band (4 for a gain over 1,000%). The need falls by up to 6 points for the same wallet, the same
/// hour and the same share band.
contract UncoveredBandDodgeTest is Fixture {
    function test_pleadingOneWeiOverTheBuyRecordNeverScoresBetterThanTheCoveredPlea() public {
        // Alice buys early; the market then runs far above her basis.
        buy(alice, 10e18);
        buy(bob, 30_000e18);
        buy(carol, 30_000e18);
        skip(1 days);
        // The next hour's first trade checkpoints the lifted price the gate scores against.
        buy(bob, 1e18);
        (uint256 spent, uint256 held,) = hook.basisOf(alice);
        assertGt(held, 0, "alice has a buy record");
        // Alice also holds PLEA no buy record covers (a season prize paid by Seasons).
        vm.prank(address(seasons));
        plea.transfer(alice, held * 3);

        uint8 covered = gate.factScore(alice, held);
        uint8 uncovered = gate.factScore(alice, held + 1);
        emit log_named_uint("imd spent", spent);
        emit log_named_uint("plea held by the record", held);
        emit log_named_uint("fact score, covered plea (amount = record)", covered);
        emit log_named_uint("fact score, plea one wei over the record", uncovered);
        // Expected: the same wallet, hour and share band cannot score better by adding one wei.
        assertLe(uncovered, covered, "one wei over the record must not improve the fact score");
    }

    function test_theLowerNeedIsRealOnAPaidPlea() public {
        buy(alice, 10e18);
        buy(bob, 30_000e18);
        buy(carol, 30_000e18);
        skip(1 days);
        buy(bob, 1e18);
        (, uint256 held,) = hook.basisOf(alice);
        vm.prank(address(seasons));
        plea.transfer(alice, held * 3);
        uint8 covered = gate.factScore(alice, held);
        uint256 id = plead(alice, held + 1);
        CabalGate.Plea memory p = gate.getPlea(id);
        emit log_named_uint("need of the covered plea would be", 71 - covered);
        emit log_named_uint("need of the paid plea one wei over the record", p.need);
        assertGe(p.need, 71 - uint256(covered), "the paid plea must not need less than the covered one");
    }
}
```

### 2. Info: The plea/objection text filter admits several invisible Unicode format (Cf) characters it does not list: U+206A-206F, U+FFF9-FFFB, U+0600-0605, U+06DD, U+070F, U+08E2

`src/CabalGate.sol:855`

```
                    if (c == 0xe2 && c1 == 0x81 && ((c2 >= 0xa0 && c2 <= 0xa4) || (c2 >= 0xa6 && c2 <= 0xa9))) {
```

Specialist (audit_flow) Info, reproduced by the judge; kept at Info. Invariant 2 promises plea text with no control, zero-width, bidi, tag or variation-selector characters. `_validateText` (and the Tribunal's identical copy at src/Tribunal.sol:519) blocks U+200B-200F, U+2028-202E, U+2060-2064, U+2066-2069, U+FEFF, U+FE00-FE0F, U+180E, U+00AD, U+061C and the U+E0000 plane, but not the rest of Unicode's format class: U+206A-206F (deprecated inhibit/activate symmetric swapping and Arabic form shaping controls, invisible), U+FFF9-FFFB (interlinear annotation anchor, separator and terminator: a renderer that honours them shows the text between the anchor and the separator and hides the text between the separator and the terminator), U+0600-0605, U+06DD, U+070F, U+08E2 (Arabic/Syriac number and end-of-ayah signs). None is zero-width or bidi in the strict sense the invariant names, so this is a filter-completeness note rather than a broken invariant: the panel reads the decoded text and the only effect is text a browser may render differently from what the judges' model reads (an annotation hidden on the site but read by the panel, or the reverse). No value path. If the filter is meant to cover Unicode Cf, add the ranges above (a few byte comparisons per class, in both validators); otherwise document that only the listed classes are refused.

Expected if format characters count as 'control' under invariant 2: revert BadPleaText. Actual: the plea is accepted and gate.pleaText(id) returns the bytes unchanged.
