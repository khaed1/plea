Context: PleaHook is a fork of POOL4's CappedBurnHook (0xc6c965bd164c483e87d0b550671798e9a3602840) on the IMD side: IMD as the quote asset, LP fee 0, the hook is the only liquidity provider and its liquidity is locked forever, trims burn 100% of the PLEA and the recovered IMD becomes an IMD-only buy wall below the price. Every trade pays 2.25% inside the swap: 0.5% cashback as staked IMD through imd/acc's Stacker (gas budget creditGas + a 1M reserve, plain IMD if the credit reverts), 1% to the owner, 0.25% to the season pot (pool liquidity once the Cabal is dead), 0.25% to pool liquidity, 0.25% of the PLEA leg burned. It keeps hourly price checkpoints for the gate (the price an hour's first trade found), each trader's cost basis and hold time, a 90-minute launch window (extra fee 70% to 0%, 5M PLEA per buy), the inventory cap ratchet (only down, at most (seeded - 900k) / 15 a day, never below the holdings or 900k) with trims, the wall placement guard (rises at most 400 ticks a day, drops at once), and the trader rule (hookData, else the router's msgSender(), else a plain-wallet sender; never a router, tx.origin, the Stacker, IMD, sIMD, the PoolManager or the hook).
Since round 7 (FINDINGS.md "Changes after round 7"): R7-A3-1 fixed: _burnPlea burns PLEA the PoolManager holds, else mints the hook ERC-6909 PLEA claims (pleaClaims) that rebalance burns (sale fee, trim, wall); R7-A3-2 fixed: the fee refund runs after _payFees. basisOf and the checkpoints feed the fact table; the Letterbox's delivery floor assumes 0.25% PLEA + 2% IMD sale fees.
