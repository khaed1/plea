FOCUS:
Context: the Letterbox turns a transaction's input data (or letter(text)) into a plea for 15% of the writer's PLEA, an APPEAL, a SELL or a NAME; it escrows the PLEA, pays the fees in PLEA through the gate's fee sale (0.5 IMD oracle fee, 0.25 IMD runner tip, an appeal's 0.35 IMD wall share; at most the snapshot value + 10% and 10% of the balance), and anyone delivers an approved plea for at least 95% of its snapshot value for the tip; the site's auto-sell box (plead / appealAuto) does the same and pays the 0.35 IMD auto-sell fee from a delivered sale. PLEA is the ERC-20 with transfer gating while the Cabal lives (system senders and the owner's add-only allowlist exempt; two-step ownership), the one-time launcher registration and the one-time mint 82 / 6 / 12. PleaLaunch registers the system at launch, commits to the hook's, the Letterbox's and the Tribunal's creation code, deploys them afterwards for anyone, and opens trading (seed, with the mainnet clock check). PleaDistributor is the Merkle claim for the 12%.
Changed since round 4 (FINDINGS.md R4-A2-*): the appeal fee exception carries the fees' 10% slack; plead refuses escrow plus fees over the balance (FeeTooHigh); new code hash in launch.json. Earlier: R1-A2-*, R2-A2-1, R3-A2-1..2. Changed since run 6: FINDINGS.md "Run 7 changes" (letters pay no auto-sell fee, LetterTooLong, pointsFor, Ownable2Step, the seat NFT and the Letterbox hash in launch.json).

- Fees in PLEA: the fee sale's bounds (+10%, 10% of the balance, an appeal up to its own amount), unspent PLEA back, the 0.70 IMD floor at the snapshot, the auto-sell fee capped at the sale and taken only from the site's box (flags), the BY_LETTER / AUTO_SELL flags and the gate's BadFlags, minting the Sepolia oracle fee vs buying it on mainnet (MintOnMainnet, OracleAssetNotImd).
- APPEAL letters: the target rule (_appealTarget), the same-share clamp, the 1% floor, the 0.70 raise, the gate's lesser-share band, re-sending an unanswered appeal.
- Text handling: command words, _after, the selector rule (no function ID is valid text), letter(text) vs raw data, LetterTooLong, reentrancy through the hook or tokens during deliver / settle.
- PLEA: every path a holder could use to move PLEA while the Cabal lives (transfer, transferFrom, the PoolManager, the gate, the Letterbox, allowlisted senders), setLauncher's registration window (the deploy transaction's flag or PLEA's deployment block), init once, burn, two-step ownership and the exclusion Seasons reads.
