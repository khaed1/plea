# PLEA run 7: threat model for auditors

Read this before the code. It says who can do what, what must always hold, and which behaviours are **deliberate** (reporting them wastes a round). Design: [`../README.md`](../README.md); Objection!: [`../OBJECTIONS.md`](../OBJECTIONS.md) (its *Known limits* 1–8 and *Decisions* table are part of section 3 below); the findings ledger: [`FINDINGS.md`](FINDINGS.md).

PLEA is a sell-gated meme token on Uniswap v4: anyone buys, selling needs a plea judged by a 30-seat IdentityMD oracle panel ("the Cabal"), approved pleas score season points for IMD and PLEA prizes, and every trade pays cashback in staked IMD through imd/acc's Stacker. Solidity 0.8.26, cancun, via-IR, no proxies, no upgrade path. The audited code is the **mainnet code**, rehearsed on Sepolia first: `launch.json` on this branch carries the Sepolia settings (5-hour seasons, the open-mint test tokens); the `mainnet` branch carries the same contracts with Ethereum's (15-day seasons, IMD `0xd34a…3b7`, imd/acc's mainnet Stacker, a 14,000 IMD seed).

**Build (important):** the whole tree compiles under via-IR as one slow solc job and two contracts sit near the EIP-170 size limit. Run `forge build --skip test --skip script` first (under a minute), then tests one group at a time with `forge test --match-path 'test/{A,B}.t.sol'`; the fork rehearsals and the fuzz-heavy suites are behind `FOUNDRY_PROFILE=full`. Never a single `forge build` of the whole tree.

## 1. Actors and what they can do

| Actor | Powers | Trust |
|---|---|---|
| Buyers and traders | Buy PLEA in the PLEA/IMD pool through any router (every buy while the Cabal lives needs a resolvable trader: `hookData = abi.encode(trader)`, else the router's `msgSender()`, else a plain-wallet sender, which in practice cannot reach the PoolManager). Receive 0.5% cashback as sIMD | Untrusted. Assume flash loans, MEV, many wallets, contracts as wallets, malicious routers and hookData |
| Sellers (pleaders) | `CabalGate.submitSell` / `appeal` with text, pay the 0.5 IMD oracle fee (an appeal also 0.35 IMD to the wall), `executeSell` inside the 7-minute window, `Seasons.record` | Untrusted. Will try to sell without a plea, sell more or later than approved, farm points, forge markers or objections, re-roll verdicts |
| Letter writers | Send text to the `Letterbox` (raw input data or `letter(text)`): a plea for 20% of their PLEA, `APPEAL`, `SELL`, `NAME`; the site's `plead` / `appealAuto`. Fees are paid in PLEA through the gate's fee sale | Untrusted. Will try to make the Letterbox move another writer's escrow or tip, pay less than the fees, or route a letter into a function |
| Runners and keepers | Every upkeep call is permissionless: `deliver`, `settle`, `expire`, `record`, `closeSeason(ids)`, `flushToWall`, `rebalance`, `Tribunal.settle` / `expire`, `deployLetterbox`, `deployTribunal`, `deployHook`, `seed` once `START` passes | Untrusted; may call in any order, at any time, repeatedly, or never. Nothing may depend on a keeper being honest |
| Objectors | `Tribunal.object(pleaId, text)` for 20 minutes after an approval: 0.5 IMD fee + 0.35 IMD stake | Untrusted. Will try to object to their own plea through a second wallet, to shield it, to re-roll a rival until it falls, to get a stake back twice |
| The PLEA owner (`PLEA.owner`, `Ownable2Step`) | `PLEA.allow(account)` (add-only transfer allowlist, empty at launch), `PleaLaunch.seed()` before `START`, `transferOwnership` (two steps). **Excluded** from points, prizes and the bonus, with the launch owner | Semi-trusted: the powers above and nothing else. A finding is any way to exceed them, or any way the team could collect a prize |
| The launch owner (`PleaHook.owner`, immutable) | Receives the hook's 0.75% owner fee. No other power. Excluded from points | As above |
| The distributor owner (`PleaDistributor.owner`, `Ownable2Step`) | `setMerkleRoot` once (the swarm's 10% and the 2% airdrop); cannot renounce before the root is set | As above; a wrong root is the owner's loss, not a contract finding |
| The IMD oracle signer (`CabalGate.oracleSigner`, immutable; the Tribunal reuses it) | Signs the panel's numeric answer (EIP-712, then ERC-1271) that the Intake delivers to `onPleaResult` / `onOracleResult` | Trusted for its answer. The contracts must bind each answer to one exact request, check panel 30 / quorum 17 / score 0–45, and use it once |
| The Intake (`CabalGate.intake`, immutable) and its writer | Takes the 0.5 IMD fee with the question; its writer (the oracle plane's key, the only caller of `complete`) delivers the signed answer or the unsigned failure to the consumer with 200k gas on mainnet, naming the request the callback is for | Trusted contract and writer: the pairing of a signed answer with the request it answers is the writer's (L85-I-1); a dishonest writer could at most re-address a signed score, never forge one. A failure callback must clear only our own pending request and never move value |
| The seat NFT (`Seasons.seat`, immutable: the IMD identity.md NFT on Ethereum, the open-mint test NFT on Sepolia) | Its `balanceOf` decides the 1.2× points multiplier at record time | Trusted contract, read fail-open with a gas cap: it must never be able to block a record or a close |
| IMD's launch factory | Deploys the five `launch.json` contracts in one atomic transaction with `$owner` resolved; rehearses the manifest on a bare EVM whose chain id is 1 | Trusted deployer. The constructors must work on any chain id with any owner |
| Outside routers and PoolManager callers | Swap through the PoolManager, unlock it, call the hook's callbacks | Untrusted. May unlock the PoolManager and call anything in the callback |

Trusted externals: the Uniswap v4 PoolManager, IMD and sIMD (and imd/acc's ownerless `Stacker.credit`, called with a gas budget and a plain-IMD fallback), the test tokens on Sepolia.

## 2. Invariants (each one broken is at least High)

Agreed numbers appear in bold; a change to any of them is a finding in itself.

**The gate and the verdict**
1. While the Cabal lives, PLEA leaves a holder's wallet only through the gate after an approval, for the approved amount, inside the **7-minute** window, in one swap through the hook; exceptions are only the system senders and the owner's add-only allowlist. Transfers to the PoolManager, the gate and the Letterbox stay restricted for everyone else. After the Cabal dies every restriction ends, forever.
2. A plea is **1%** of the balance or more (or **2.5M PLEA** when that is less), at most **2.5M PLEA** and **50%** of the balance, **1–280 bytes** of well-formed UTF-8 with no control or format (Unicode Cf: zero-width, bidi, tags, the Arabic and Syriac number signs, the annotation and other invisible controls) characters, no line or paragraph separator, no variation selector and no other default-ignorable character (rounds 7 and 8, R7-A1-2, R8-A1-1: Unicode 15.1's Cc, Cf, Zl, Zp, Variation_Selector and Default_Ignorable_Code_Point above U+007F, 4,240 code points), and no `[PLEA` / `[/PLEA` / `[OBJECTION` / `[/OBJECTION` marker prefix in any case. It costs exactly the **0.5 IMD** oracle fee paid to the Intake (an appeal also **0.35 IMD** to the buy wall); the gate holds no funds between transactions beyond the plea in flight.
3. Need = **71 − fact score**; the fact score (0–45: share band **≤20%: 16 · ≤35%: 9 · more: 3**, exact edges (run 8.5, L85-M-1); hold **≥7d: 7 · ≥3d: 4 · ≥1d: 2**; **at a loss 13 · gain ≤50%: 12 · ≤200%: 10 · ≤500%: 8 · ≤1,000%: 6 · more: 4**; PLEA no buy record covers is held since trading opened at profit **10**, and a plea larger than the buy record takes the PLEA-weighted mean of the record's hold and profit points and those of received PLEA, rounded to the nearest point (rounds 7 and 8, R7-A1-1, R8-A1-2); 24h trend **up 9 · flat or no history 5 · down 0**) is computed at submit time from the hook's **hourly checkpoints** (the price an hour's first trade *found*), never from spot. An appeal's share band is scored on the lesser of its own share and the original plea's. A plea whose need exceeds 45 is refused before it is paid for (`CannotPass`).
4. A verdict is accepted only from the oracle signer, for the request id the gate paid for (the Intake v2's writer, the only caller of its `complete`, binds the signed attestation to that request's question when it delivers the callback: the Intake contract forwards the writer's arguments as they are, the attestation's `requestId` is the plane's own request id rather than the Intake's, and the gate does not re-hash the question on chain; run 8.5, L85-I-1), with **panel 30, quorum 17, score 0–45**, once; approval iff **score ≥ need**. The failure function clears only the plea whose request failed and moves no value; an unanswered plea expires after **20 minutes** (no cooldown, no fee back). Pending expiry and the callback are the only ways out of `Pending`.
5. A sale, a denial or a lapse starts a **4-hour** cooldown (a sale's and a denial's from that moment, a lapse's from the verdict that lapsed, so 3 h 53 min after the window closed); one appeal inside it, for the same or a smaller amount; an appeal that got no verdict may be sent again. Lapsed approvals still count for the season.
6. The dead-man: **33 hours** without a signed plea verdict kills the Cabal for good; `cabalAlive()` is computed, never stored; objection rulings never reset it; a late callback cannot revive it. On chain id 1 `PleaLaunch.seed` refuses seasons under 15 days, a dead-man under 33 hours and a hook whose opening market cap (`MARKET_CAP_IMD`) is under **14,000 IMD** (round 8, R8-A3-1).

**Seasons and prizes**
7. A plea's points are its recorded score, i.e. min(original score, lowest re-score) (0–45), times **1.2 rounded down** when the seller holds at least one seat NFT at record time (binary: 45 → 54; `Seasons.pointsFor`); points only, never the score the Tribunal compares with the need. Each plea is recorded once, by anyone, in the season of its verdict, only after its objection window and any pending objection, never after a sustain; each wallet's **best three** pleas count; ties favour the earlier plea.
8. `closeSeason` closes seasons in order, each only after it ended plus the **1-hour** grace, and pays the **top 25** with at least **60 points** shares of (that season's fees + rollover) from **450 down to 350 bps** in a straight line (`shareBps`, sum 10,000); unpaid shares roll over; the pot never pays more than it holds (`accountedPot`). The **PLEA bonus**, **10,000,000 PLEA** for each of the **first six** seasons from the 6% minted to `Seasons`, follows the same table and bar, and every unpaid share is burned at the close. Nobody can withdraw IMD or PLEA from `Seasons`; after the death and the last close `flushToWall` sends what is left to the hook's wall and burns the rest of the bonus.
9. The PLEA owner and the launch owner never earn points, prizes or the bonus: `record` refuses them, `closeSeason` drops them (their share rolls over / burns). A nominated-but-unaccepted owner is not excluded.
10. Reads of the Tribunal (`standing`), of PLEA's launcher, of the hook's `owner()` and of the seat NFT are fail-open: a missing, reverting, gas-hungry or malformed contract can never block `record`, `closeSeason` or `flushToWall`; it only means "no hold / no exclusion / no multiplier".

**Objection! (the Tribunal)**
11. An objection is filed only inside **20 minutes** after an approval, by a wallet that is neither the seller nor excluded, on a plea not yet recorded and not sustained, while rulings plus pending objections are fewer than **two**, by a wallet other than the one ruled and the one pending; it costs the **0.5 IMD** oracle fee and a **0.35 IMD** stake. Text rules as for pleas. Two objections may be pending at once (round 5, R5-A1-1); one still pending after a sustain is ruled on its own and never reopens the case.
12. A ruling is accepted under the Tribunal's own EIP-712 domain with the gate's checks (signer, exact question, panel 30, quorum 17, 0–45), once. **Sustained** iff `newScore + 2 < need` (the plea's stored need): no points this season, stake back. **Overruled** otherwise: recorded score = min(original, lowest), stake to the **plea's season** pot (rollover if closed) through `Seasons.checkpointObjection`, which only the committed Tribunal can call and only for IMD it has already transferred. **Void** (refused, split, or 20 minutes passed): stake back, slot freed. Stakes are conserved: every objection's stake leaves the Tribunal exactly once, to the objector or to Seasons.
13. The sale is never touched by an objection; the hold delays only the recording of points, and window + expiry (40 minutes) always ends inside the close grace.

**Letters (the Letterbox)**
14. The Letterbox's accounting is exactly the PLEA of its open escrows plus one **0.25 IMD** tip per open escrow, and it never pays out anything else (PLEA parked in it by a buy that names it as the trader is inert: section 3, sinks); every escrow ends exactly once: sold through `deliver` / `SELL` only after an approval inside the window for at least **95%** of the hourly-snapshot value, otherwise returned whole with its tip through `settle` (a lapsed approval is recorded first). No writer's PLEA moves except through their own letters; a stranger can only deliver or settle, never redirect.
15. A letter pleads **20%** of the writer's PLEA (≥ **1,000 PLEA**, ≤ 2.5M); its fees are paid in PLEA through the gate's fee sale at most the snapshot value **+10%**, never more than **10%** of the balance (an appeal: or the original plea's amount with the fees' own 10% slack), and unspent PLEA comes straight back. **A letter pays no auto-sell fee**; the site's auto-sell box (`plead` / `appealAuto`) pays **0.35 IMD** of a delivered sale (never more than the sale) to the season pot, only from a sale. Every plea through the Letterbox must be worth at least **0.70 IMD** at the snapshot. An `APPEAL` letter keeps the original's share (the gate's lesser-share band) and is raised to the 0.70 IMD floor's worth when the clamp lands under it, never above the original or 50% of the balance.
16. No public function selector of the Letterbox can be the first four bytes of valid letter text; letters never carry ETH; a text over **280 bytes** is refused with `LetterTooLong(bytes, 280)`; all entry points are non-reentrant.

**Launch, token, distributor**
17. The five `launch.json` contracts deploy atomically in order with the listed constructor arguments on any chain id; `PleaLaunch` registers them with PLEA in its constructor and commits to the hook's, the Letterbox's and the Tribunal's creation code; it refuses the oracle-fee settings the Letterbox would refuse on every chain (a foreign asset not minted, the pool IMD minted, an asset other than the gate's), and `seed` waits for the Letterbox and for a committed Tribunal (round 5, R5-A2-1; round 6); it refuses a zero Letterbox hash or one equal to the Tribunal's (R6-A2-1): whoever calls `deployHook` / `deployLetterbox` / `deployTribunal` gets exactly the committed code at a determined address, once each; a zero Tribunal hash means objections off for good. PLEA mints its **1,000,000,000** supply exactly once, at `init`: **82%** to the hook, **6%** to Seasons, **12%** to the distributor. `seed` opens trading once (owner any time, anyone from `START`); the launch fits one Ethereum transaction (16,777,216 gas with IMD's 22% headroom; `test/LaunchManifest.t.sol`).
18. `PleaDistributor`: one root, set once; a claim pays only a listed (index, account, amount) leaf, once, to that account; ownership moves in two steps and cannot be renounced before the root is set.

**The market (the hook)**
19. Every trade through any router pays exactly **2.0%** on the filled amount, settled inside the swap: on the IMD leg **0.5%** cashback (sIMD through the Stacker, plain IMD if the credit reverts or the Stacker has no code, nothing without a resolved trader), **0.75%** to the owner, **0.25%** to the season pot (pool liquidity once the Cabal is dead), **0.25%** to pool liquidity (the wall); **0.25%** of the PLEA leg burned. A partial fill of an exact-input trade pays only on what filled and refunds the rest of the reserved fee after the payouts (an ERC-6909 claim when the PoolManager cannot pay it then; round 7, R7-A3-2); an exact-output trade fills whole or reverts (`PartialFill`); both currency orderings return exact deltas. The first buy into an empty PoolManager may route its payouts to pool liquidity instead of reverting.
20. The hook is the pool's only liquidity provider and its liquidity can never be withdrawn; nobody else can add liquidity or initialize the pool. For **90 minutes** after `seed` buys pay an extra fee falling from **70% to 0%** of the IMD that reaches the pool (41.8% of the IMD sent at the open; to pool liquidity) and are capped at **5M PLEA** each (per swap: a router may chain swaps in one transaction, so the cap bounds one swap's price impact, not one buyer's total). The pool opens at a **5,700 IMD** market cap on Sepolia (**14,000** on mainnet).
21. The inventory cap starts at the seeded PLEA and only ever falls, by at most **(seeded − 900,000) ÷ 15** PLEA per day, never below what the market holds and never below **900,000 PLEA**. PLEA sold back above the cap is trimmed: burned, with the matching IMD moved to the wall; PLEA the PoolManager cannot pay before the seller settles (a sale larger than the pool's PLEA, the trim or the 0.25% fee) is kept as ERC-6909 PLEA claims (`pleaClaims`) and burned at the next `rebalance`, so no sale through a swap-then-settle router reverts for it (round 7, R7-A3-1). The buy wall is placed only below the guard price (which drops at once to any lower traded price and rises at most **400 ticks** a day), its IMD never leaves the pool, `rebalance` pays no tip, and a cashback credit can never reach the wall reserve (the Stacker allowance is per credit).
22. A swap must reach the hook with `creditGas` plus the **1M** reserve or it reverts (`NotEnoughGas`), so no gas limit can skip the cashback. Cost basis and buy-weighted hold time are kept per trader and reduced only by final sales; escrowed letter PLEA keeps its record.

## 3. Deliberate behaviour: do not report

- **OBJECTIONS.md *Known limits* 1–8** (two sham objections can exhaust the two slots, including by two friendly wallets: the seller shield; noisy re-scoring; points arrive up to 40 minutes later; an unanswered panel still spends the fee; objection text reaches the judges; forgery refused at the gate; permissionless settlement with no keeper tip; every objection is a re-roll that can only lower the score). All accepted in writing (run 6, user 2026-10-14).
- **Min-of-panels.** The recorded score is the minimum of the original and every re-score, so a cheap objection has positive expected value against a leader (run-5 economics info, our audit M2). Accepted: the two-ruling cap, the 0.85 IMD price and the stake lost on an overrule are the brakes; watched in the test run.
- **The owner is excluded** (points, prizes, bonus), with the launch owner, so the team can never collect a prize; the **exclusion is read at record time and at the close**, not at the verdict: a plea approved while its seller owned PLEA records after a handover (run-6 permissions low; nominal: the launch owner stays excluded forever, a second wallet achieves the same).
- **The hard-coded 0.5 IMD Intake price and action id** in the gate and the ownerless Tribunal: a change of IMD's price disables pleas or objections for good (run-5 economics info, accepted in run 4).
- **Letter points are recorded later by anyone** when a Tribunal exists (`deliver` sells inside the hold; `Seasons.record` afterwards, or a close's id list). A keeper does it; a season closed with an empty list drops the points of pleas nobody recorded (run-5 permissions info).
- **The 0.35 IMD overruled stake goes to the plea's season** (rollover if that season is closed); sustained and void stakes come back; the oracle fee is always spent.
- **The seat multiplier is binary and read at record time**: ten seats count as one; a wallet that holds a seat only for the recording transaction gets the multiplier (`record` is permissionless: the first caller, not the seller, fixes the moment, so a seat must sit in the selling wallet from the verdict on, and a rival may record a seatless seller at the first eligible second); a seat sold after the recording keeps the points. The multiplier never touches the need, the Tribunal's comparison or the 60-point bar.
- **Points only go down on objection**; a seller's second wallet objecting to its own plea costs 0.85 IMD and can only lower its points (OBJECTIONS *Not included*: backfire).
- **The PLEA owner's add-only allowlist** and **seeding early** are listed powers. **Anyone may `seed` from `START`**. The owner can never change the signer, the Intake, fees, timings, prizes or the shares.
- **The opening buys: while this pool's own IMD (the market band and the wall before the swap, plus the hook's claims) plus the buy's own IMD fee is under 2 IMD (`PAYOUT_RESERVE`)**, a buy's unfunded cashback, owner and prize shares join pool liquidity instead of reverting; no flag, so no dust buy can use the exception up. The PoolManager's balance is never consulted (round 3, R3-A3-1: a router could flash-take it inside its own unlock), so a drained PoolManager only makes that router's swap revert at the hook's take. **A later buy larger than about 66× the pool's own IMD** pays those shares as ERC-6909 IMD claims on the PoolManager to the same beneficiaries (`CashbackPaidClaims`; Seasons redeems its own at a close; a wallet needs a contract that unlocks, burns and takes to redeem; no sIMD for that buy). Only the first few sizeable buys after the open can hit it; the launch owner's own 2 IMD buy right after `seed` (the window caps a swap at 5M PLEA, whose payouts are under 1.05 IMD) closes it: the next buy's own fee lifts the sum over the reserve although rounding leaves the pool itself a few wei under 2 IMD (round 4, R4-A3-2). A partial fill's unearned fee goes back to the trader, or to the router when no trader resolved (after the death only). The same claims path pays **the refund of a partially filled exact-input buy** (a trader's own price limit near spot: the unearned fee on the unfilled part comes back as ERC-6909 IMD claims whenever it exceeds the PoolManager's IMD), at any pool age; the trader chose the limit.
- **A buy in another wallet's name moves that wallet's buy-weighted holding time** (the hook delivers the PLEA to the named trader and records the buy in its history; it cannot tell who paid): a seller a few hours past a hold-time band edge can be nudged under it by a gift worth about 0.6% of their position per hour past the edge, costing them up to 3 need points on that plea. Accepted by the owner (round 2, R2-A3-2): the gift is real and stays with the victim, and a day's wait defeats it.
- **A buy while the Cabal lives returns a zero PLEA delta to the swapper** (the hook delivers the PLEA to the trader itself), so a public router cannot enforce an output minimum; slippage protection is the swap's price limit, which the site's router passes. A sandwich needs an approved plea inside its 7-minute window for the back leg.
- **The cap's daily allowance banks over idle days** (a quiet stretch lets one buy ratchet the cap by several days' worth at once; the floor and the 15-day pace are unchanged), unlike the wall guard, whose idle time is capped at one day.
- **The opening market cap is at least the stated one, within one 60-tick spacing (0.6%)**: `seed` aligns the single-sided band to the tick grid toward the PLEA-only side (5,720.83 IMD for the 5,700 setting; the same rounding on the 14,000 mainnet setting).
- **Sinks.** Tokens sent straight to the hook, the gate, `Seasons` (IMD donations are accounted to the running season at the next `checkpoint`, PLEA sent to Seasons joins the bonus and burns), the Letterbox, the Tribunal or the distributor stay there or follow those contracts' accounting; only the sender's own funds are lost. A buy whose hookData names a system contract (the Letterbox, Seasons, the Tribunal, the distributor, or the PLEA token itself) delivers the bought PLEA (and, for the token, the sIMD cashback) into it, where it is inert: the Letterbox pays out only its escrow accounting (invariant 14), so no writer's escrow or tip moves; the gate refuses to be named (round 4, R4-A2-4).
- **An unanswered letter returns its tip**: a letter's fee sale buys 0.75 IMD of fees on mainnet (0.5 oracle fee + 0.25 tip, +10% at most) and the 0.25 IMD tip comes back to the writer on every non-sale ending, so a letter the panel never answers (expired after 20 minutes, no cooldown) converts about 0.825 IMD worth of PLEA into 0.25 IMD every 20 minutes at the writer's own two-thirds loss; the oracle fee is always spent. On Sepolia the oracle fee is minted, so the sale buys only the tip. A last-resort exit, not a leak (round 4, R4-A2-5).
- **A letter's own fee sale can be the hour's first trade** and then writes that hour's checkpoint at the price it found (the rule above), so an `APPEAL` letter sent in the same hour is scored on the new P&L and trend facts; the share band is kept (the lesser-share rule), the need moves by at most the price bands (round 4, R4-A2-6).
- **An exact-output trade fills whole or reverts** (`PartialFill`): its fee is reserved on the requested output, and a price-limited partial fill would otherwise hand the swapper a short, even negative, output delta with the rest as a separate refund. Exact-output buys are refused while the Cabal lives anyway; after the death a router must not price-limit an exact-output trade (round 4, R4-A3-1). Exact-input partial fills pay on the fill and refund the rest of the reserved fee after the payouts: an IMD refund the PoolManager cannot pay is an ERC-6909 claim, a PLEA refund is always ERC-20 (a PLEA claim could be sold in a hookless pool while the Cabal lives), so after the death a price-limited sale whose PLEA refund exceeds the PLEA the PoolManager holds before the seller settles (a market nearly bought out) reverts and is retried without the limit (round 7).
- **`pendingRebalance()` is a hint** (round 8): while a post-death sale has drained the pool to a price limit beyond the market band (no active liquidity), a reserve alone makes the view true, but `rebalance()` places no wall and reverts `RebalanceNotNeeded` when that is all it could do; a keeper simulates the call first. Making the view exact would take about 104 of the hook's 174 spare bytes.
- **A flash-taken PoolManager defers the PLEA burn** (round 8): after the death a router may take the PoolManager's PLEA inside its own unlock, so its swap's 0.25% burn (and any trim) is kept as ERC-6909 PLEA claims instead of burned in the swap; the claims are backed, `pendingRebalance()` turns true and the next `rebalance()` burns them; supply plus `totalBurned` is conserved and nobody loses value (`test_aFlashTakenPoolManagerOnlyDefersTheBurn`). While the Cabal lives the router cannot settle PLEA back, so the path is closed.
- **Scores are subjective** and a split panel gives no verdict with the fee spent; the dead-man is the exit. The appeal question tells the judges the real previous score and need (rules v3).
- **The hourly checkpoints live in a 48-slot ring** (two days): the day-old reference the trend band reads survives up to 23 quiet hours, and the current hour's first trade can only evict the slot of 48 hours ago (round 3, R3-A1-1: with 25 slots a dust trade evicted the only day-old checkpoint and a down trend read as "no history").
- **One trade per hour sets no reference**: the hourly checkpoint records the price the hour's first trade found, i.e. the price the previous hour's last trade left; a pump placed as the last trade before the hour boundary becomes the reference for the next hour's pleas (the pumper keeps PLEA it cannot sell and worsens its own P&L band; at most the trend band moves).
- **Trades without a resolvable trader** go through without cashback and without a buy record (the 0.5% stays in the pool); a buy while the Cabal lives needs a recipient, so it reverts `RecipientRequired`.
- **Prize PLEA and claimed distributor PLEA** are ordinary PLEA with no buy record: selling them takes a plea, scored (when no buy record covers it) as held since trading opened at profit 10 (run 8); a plea larger than the buy record is scored part by part and takes the PLEA-weighted mean, rounded to the nearest point, so one wei over the record changes nothing in either direction (rounds 7 and 8, R7-A1-1, R8-A1-2); the rounding can move a mixed plea half a point toward the seller.
- **A letter whose text starts with a command word and a space** (`APPEAL `, `NAME `, `SELL`) is that command, never a plea; any other first word is a plea (the README's letter table).
- **The Letterbox constructor's one chain-dependent refusal** (the mint flag on chain id 1) cannot sit in `PleaLaunch`'s constructor, because IMD's launch rehearsal deploys every manifest on chain id 1, Sepolia's included (run 5 was parked that way). Such a launch deploys, but `deployLetterbox` reverts and `seed` refuses until a Letterbox exists, so trading never opens and nobody's IMD is in it; the release branch's manifest test also deploys the Letterbox through `PleaLaunch` on chain id 1 with that manifest's own values. The chain-independent refusals are now in `PleaLaunch`'s constructor (R5-A2-1, fixed after round 5).

## 4. Severity

| Severity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Critical | Anyone can steal or permanently freeze user, prize or pool funds, sell without a plea while the Cabal lives, remove the locked liquidity, or mint or inflate supply |
| High | Loss or theft under realistic conditions; breaks an invariant in section 2; lets the owner, the launch owner, the distributor owner, a keeper or the signer exceed its bounds; a changed agreed number |
| Medium | Bounded loss, griefing that costs the attacker less than the victim, wrong accounting with a limited effect, a wrong verdict path, DoS of a non-critical path (points, a display view, a season close that a later call repairs) |
| Low | Edge cases with no realistic loss, missing checks with no impact today, display text |
| Info | Style, gas, docs, tests |

A round is **clean** when all three jobs' judges report no open Critical or High. Every Medium is fixed or accepted by the project owner in writing in `FINDINGS.md`.
